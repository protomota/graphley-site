Privacy

Graphley is a Project ATOM project. Your numbers stay on your devices. Updated November 27, 2026.

The app

Graphley stores your workspace (the sites, apps and dashboards you set up) and the numbers it fetches on your Mac. It fetches them directly from the services you connect, using your own sign-in and keys: Google Analytics, Google Search Console, App Store Connect, Bing Webmaster Tools and Instagram. It also reads public App Store listings and public YouTube channel feeds for the apps and channels you add. Graphley has no server and no account, and sends nothing to us.

Sign-in tokens and keys are kept in the Keychain. If you use iCloud Keychain, they are available on your other Macs.

To check for updates, Graphley downloads a small feed from graphley.ai. The request carries the app and macOS version, as any web request does, and no analytics data.

iPhone and iCloud

When you use the iPhone and iPad app, the Mac writes a snapshot of your numbers to your private iCloud database so your iPhone can show them. Only you can read it. Keys and tokens are never part of the snapshot.

Local agent connections

The MCP server runs on your Mac when an agent starts it, and works on the same local workspace. You choose whether agents can only view, or can also make changes, and whether they can save keys. Changes made by agents are listed in Settings → Agents.

Your chosen agent may send information it retrieves to its AI provider. Review that provider’s privacy terms before connecting.

Where we host

This site runs on Cloudflare. Cloudflare serves every page and manages our traffic, so it handles standard request details such as your IP address and browser to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. Our images and downloads are stored in Cloudflare R2. Cloudflare handles that data under its privacy policy.

We’re not a Cloudflare sponsor. We chose them because we think they’re the best value and the best service, and we’ll keep using them for as long as that stays true.

This website

graphley.ai has no sign-up form and no account. If you click Allow analytics, the site loads Google Analytics, which sets cookies to count visits, page views and link clicks. Page addresses are sent without query strings, and advertising features are off. If you decline, Google Analytics does not load. Change your choice any time with Analytics settings in the footer.

Feedback and support

Help and feedback for Graphley go through the feedback form at projectatom.ai. We receive the app you pick, your message and, only if you add it, your email address. The note is emailed to our team through Cloudflare Email. If you leave an email, we use it to reply and follow up. If you leave it out, we can’t reply.

The app never sends a report on its own. Only what you type into the form is sent, so please leave private files and details out of it.

Questions and changes

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t use it for advertising. To ask what we hold, or to have it corrected or deleted, email hello@projectatom.ai or use the feedback form. We answer within 30 days.

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
